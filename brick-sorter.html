Brick Sorter

Photograph a pile, get a parts list

Each photo is split into individual items, then each one is identified by Brickognize: parts with their likely colour, and minifigures as whole figures. Check the guesses and add them to your inventory.

Sets in this pile optional

Add the sets you think these parts came from. Guesses that fit them are preferred and colours are limited to the ones each part comes in. Parts from other sets are still accepted, but flagged for checking.

Adding sets needs a free Rebrickable API key.

Part finder

How each photo is split into separate items. The background method needs items spread out on a plain background. Your brick detector also separates parts that touch. The figure finder picks out minifigures, even standing on a display shelf.

What's in these photos?

Check and add

Tick the loose head, torso, legs and other parts of one figure, then find which minifigure they come from. Printed torsos and heads are the best clues.

Sort into sets

Point your phone at a tray of spread-out parts. When the picture is still, each new part is identified and labelled with the bin it goes in: one bin for each set you're rebuilding, then rare parts, minifigure parts and everything else.

Starting…
  • Phone on a standPointing straight down, so the picture stays still. A held phone keeps moving; tap Scan now instead.
  • Sieve by size firstSort a scoop into small, medium and large before scanning. Similar sizes spread out more evenly.
  • Spread, scan, sweepSpread 5 to 20 parts with gaps, wait for the labels, sweep each part into its bin, then spread the next lot.

Can I build it?

Enter a set number to compare its parts list from Rebrickable with your inventory. Spare parts are left out.

Pieces are shared out in the order the sets are listed, so none is counted twice.

Which sets is this from?

Finds sets that contain your inventory's rarer parts and minifigures, then checks the best matches against their full parts lists. Common bricks are in thousands of sets, so they count for little. The first run takes a few minutes; Rebrickable lookups are remembered for next time. On a phone, keep this page on screen while it runs: switching apps or locking the phone pauses it.

Set checks need a free Rebrickable API key.
